Why this project exists

Investing websites often use unfamiliar words without explaining them. PlainStock turns common price measures into short, ordinary explanations for people who are still learning.

What makes the view different

The score describes only the market price: its direction, trading activity, position within the past year, and how bumpy the journey has been. The method is visible so you can question it.

A trend is not a personal decision.

PlainStock does not know your savings, debts, goals, other investments, or comfort with loss. It cannot decide what you personally should buy or sell.
