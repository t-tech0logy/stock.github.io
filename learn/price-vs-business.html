Research foundations

A strong chart is not the same as a strong business.

Price trend, company quality, and valuation answer three separate questions. Sound research keeps them separate before combining them.

Meaning: the chart may look strong while the business or price paid still deserves caution. Research the disagreement.

Price asks what the market is doing

A price trend simply shows whether buyers and sellers have pushed the price up, down, or sideways. It does not explain why.

Business quality asks what the company is producing

Check whether sales, profit, and cash are growing, whether debt is manageable, and whether customers still want the product.

Valuation asks what is already priced in

Valuation asks whether the share price already expects excellent results. A good company can still be too expensive.

Three independent questions

A rising price, a healthy company, and a fair price are different things. Check all three before reaching a conclusion.
