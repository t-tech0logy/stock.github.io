Portfolio foundations

One good idea should not control your future.

Diversification spreads exposure across different sources of return and risk. It cannot remove market loss, but it can reduce dependence on one company or story.

Spread your risk

Diversification means not depending too heavily on one company, industry, or country. Owning ten technology stocks may still leave you exposed to the same problem.

Position size matters

Position size means how much of your money is in one investment. The larger the position, the more one mistake can hurt the whole portfolio.

Important: spreading risk can reduce company-specific harm, but the whole market may still fall together.

Diversification is a portfolio decision.

A dashboard looking at one stock cannot see your other investments, debts, home, job, or future cash needs.

What diversification cannot promise

Different investments can still fall together during a crisis. Diversification reduces some risks; it does not remove all loss.

Questions before selecting a stock

  • How much of my money already depends on this industry?
  • Could the same event hurt both this investment and my job?
  • When might the money be needed?
  • Could a broad fund spread the risk more simply?

PlainStock can describe one investment. It cannot decide how much of your money belongs in it.
