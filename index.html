      Transparent by design How is this view calculated?

      The score uses free market data only. It checks recent and longer price direction, compares the result with the S&P 500, checks whether trading activity supports the move, and considers yearly position and price bumpiness. It does not use company profit, debt, cash flow, or valuation ratios.

      70–100 Price BUY signal 50–69 Price WAIT signal Below 50 Price AVOID signal
      Recent price trend · 25% Longer price trend · 25% Compared with the S&P 500 · 20% Trading activity · 10% Yearly range position · 10% Stability · 10%

      This is a price-movement signal, not a complete company analysis or personal financial advice. Always check the business separately before making a decision.
