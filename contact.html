Found something we should check?

Report a data problem, unclear explanation, accessibility issue, privacy concern, or responsible partnership enquiry.

Open a project issue

The most reliable contact route during the public-testing stage is the project's GitHub issue tracker. Do not include API keys, account credentials, private financial information, or other sensitive data.

Open a PlainStock issue

What to include

  • The affected page or stock symbol.
  • The date and approximate time of the issue.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot with personal information removed, when useful.

Corrections

For a factual correction, link to the original company filing, regulator document, exchange notice, or other authoritative source whenever possible. Material corrections should be prioritized over stylistic requests.

Advertising and partnerships

Paid relationships must be clearly labelled and must not influence the scoring method. PlainStock does not accept payment to change a trend score or disguise promotional content as independent research.
