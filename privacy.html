Privacy

Privacy policy.

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use PlainStock and how future advertising will be disclosed.

Information you choose to submit

Stock symbols, search terms, and assistant questions are processed to provide the requested feature. Do not place confidential, identifying, account, or financial information in the assistant.

Browser storage

PlainStock may temporarily store assistant responses and interface preferences in your browser using session storage. Session storage normally disappears when the browser tab or session is closed. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Service providers

Requests may be processed by configured market-data, AI, hosting, security, and performance providers. These services may receive technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information, requested symbol, or assistant text as necessary to operate and protect the service. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Operational logs

Hosting infrastructure may record short-lived security and diagnostic logs. These can include IP address, user agent, requested path, response status, and timing. Logs are used to prevent abuse, investigate errors, and maintain availability.

Analytics and advertising

The current site does not activate advertising merely because an advertising space exists in the layout. Before personalized advertising or non-essential analytics are enabled, this policy will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be provided where required.

If advertising is introduced, advertising partners may use cookies, IP addresses, web beacons, or similar identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and—only where permitted and consented—personalization. Users will be offered applicable privacy choices.

Data sales and sensitive information

PlainStock does not ask users to submit brokerage credentials or detailed personal financial profiles. The project does not sell personal information submitted through the dashboard.

Your choices

You can avoid the assistant, clear browser storage, reject non-essential consent choices when offered, or stop using the site. For a privacy question or deletion request concerning information under PlainStock's control, use the contact page.

Changes

This policy may change as hosting, analytics, advertising, or service providers change. The effective date below will be updated when material changes are made.
